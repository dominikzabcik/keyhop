Fair questions

What is Keyhop, exactly?

A small app on your own computer. It sits in the menu bar or tray, answers as the keyhop command, holds the logins you already have for each AI coding tool, and hands a tool another of your accounts when you ask or when the one in use runs out.

Does it share accounts or add usage?

No. Keyhop only switches between accounts you own. It cannot raise a plan's limits, and every provider's terms still apply.

What leaves my computer?

Nothing, until you link GitHub. Then daily totals per tool go to the leaderboard, and current limits only if you share them. Prompts, code and logins never do.

How do I install it?

On macOS and Linux:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dominikzabcik/keyhop/main/install.sh | bash

On Windows, in PowerShell:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dominikzabcik/keyhop/main/install.ps1 | iex

What does API value mean?

The tokens you used, priced at each provider's standard API rates. It is the value of your usage, not a bill from your subscription.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Keyhop is MIT licensed, its installers are readable shell scripts, and the app, the website, the packaging and the tests are in one public repository.
