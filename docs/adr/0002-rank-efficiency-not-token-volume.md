# Seasons and leaderboards rank efficiency, not token volume

Through 0.14.x the leaderboard ranked raw token spend. V2 flips the default to efficiency (outcome per spend: tokens per merged unit of work, cache-hit, streaks) with plan-based divisions, and adds server-side verification of uploads (token math, cost floors and ceilings). Raw tokens stay available as one optional metric.

Why: competitor research (2026-10) showed loud backlash against spend rankings ("tokenmaxxing", whale accounts dominating viberank) and distrust of unverified numbers; ranking skill instead of burn was an unserved gap. The pet keeps its lifetime-token stages — its pose and markings were never spend-based, and a lifetime creature is not a competition.
